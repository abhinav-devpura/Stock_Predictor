Backtest — growth of $1 in each long-short book

Non-overlapping rebalances every ~ trading days, top decile minus bottom decile. This is the market-neutral spread, not a total-return strategy.

Where the edge comes from — the three legs

Growth of $1 held long in each book. The long-short curve above is exactly top minus bottom. The strategy buys the top decile and shorts the bottom decile, so the gap between the green and red lines — over and above the market (grey) — is the edge.
